# Customer Churn & Retention Strategy Analysis

Business Analyst case study focused on customer churn, revenue at risk, behavioural signals, customer segmentation, and retention strategy.

## Business Objective

Identify which customers are most likely to leave, understand the business drivers behind churn, quantify revenue exposure, and prioritise retention actions.

## Key Questions
- Which customer segments have the highest churn?
- What behaviours are associated with churn?
- How much recurring revenue is at risk?
- Which high-value customers deserve immediate intervention?
- Which retention actions should be prioritised?

## Deliverables
- Synthetic customer dataset
- SQL business analysis
- Python/Pandas analysis
- Customer segmentation and churn KPIs
- Power BI dashboard specification
- Retention recommendations

## Tech Stack
Excel | SQL/MySQL | Python/Pandas | Power BI | DAX

## Data Note
This is a synthetic portfolio case study created for business-analysis demonstration. Customer and revenue figures are illustrative.
