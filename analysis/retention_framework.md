# Retention Strategy Framework

## Core KPIs
- Active Customers
- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate %
- Monthly Recurring Revenue
- Revenue at Risk
- Average Revenue per Account
- Support Contacts
- Usage Decline

## Segmentation
Prioritise customers by a combination of value, engagement, tenure, service friction, and churn behaviour.

## Suggested Action Matrix
- High value + high risk: immediate proactive retention
- High value + medium risk: targeted engagement
- Low value + high risk: low-cost automated intervention
- Low risk: maintain standard lifecycle communication

## Business Principle
The objective is not to eliminate every churn event. The objective is to allocate retention effort where expected customer value at risk is highest.
